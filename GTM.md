# GTM.md

## Exact Target User

**Engineering Manager or CTO at a 5–30 person seed or Series A startup.**

More specifically: the person at a 12-person SaaS company who approved both the Antigravity Business rollout and the ChatGPT Team subscription without anyone checking if they overlap. They run payroll, they approve SaaS invoices, and they vaguely know they're spending $3k+/month on AI tools — but they haven't sat down to audit it because the tools "just work" and there's always something more urgent.

Secondary: the solo technical founder who scaled from 1 to 5 engineers and never revisited the AI tool stack.

**What they're NOT:** enterprise procurement, non-technical founders, or companies >100 people (they have dedicated finance/procurement).

## First 100 Users in 30 Days — $0 Budget

**Day 1–5: Post on Hacker News**
Submit as "Show HN: I built a free AI spend auditor for startups — Antigravity/Copilot/Claude." The hook: most 10-person teams I talked to were paying $800–$1,200/mo with $300+ in unnecessary seats. Post at 9am ET Tuesday or Wednesday. Reply to every comment personally.

Expected: 50–200 upvotes, 500–2000 unique visitors, ~30–60 audits completed.

**Day 3–7: Post in targeted subreddits**
- r/ExperiencedDevs: "I built this after realizing our 8-person team was paying for 12 Antigravity Business seats. Audited 15 teams this week — average waste was $340/mo."
- r/startups: same hook, frame around burn rate
Be specific, include real (anonymized) examples, link to a shared audit.

**Day 5–14: Cold DM on X**
Find EMs and CTOs who tweeted about Antigravity, Copilot, or AI tool costs in the past 30 days. DM 10–15/day: "Hey — I saw your tweet about Antigravity pricing. Built a free audit tool this week that found $[amount] in savings for three teams similar to yours. Would love your honest take." Expect 5–10% response rate.

**Day 7–14: Slack drops**
Post in 3–4 engineering Slack communities with direct share links to anonymized real audit results. The share URL with concrete savings numbers does the selling: "A 10-person team just used this, saved $340/mo — here's their actual report."

**Day 14–30: Email to personal network**
Send one cold email to 30 engineers/EMs in your network. Subject: "Quick favor — does this save you money?" Include the tool link and one example result. Ask for 5 minutes and honest feedback.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex's existing customer list. Credex already has relationships with startups that buy discounted AI credits — those same companies are exactly the ones overpaying on seat-based plans. A single email to existing Credex customers ("we built a free audit tool for your team") converts at a much higher rate than cold distribution because trust is already established.

The second unfair channel: shareable audit URLs. Every audit completed creates a public page with real numbers. When that page gets posted on Hacker News or Slack ("here's what we were wasting"), it's the most credible ad possible — a real company's real spend, not a marketing claim.

## Week-1 Traction If This Works

- 80+ audits completed
- 25+ email captures (30% of audit completers)
- 5+ "high value" flags (>$500/mo savings — these become Credex consultation targets)
- 1 Hacker News post with >100 points
- 2–3 organic shares of public audit URLs on Twitter/X

The signal we're watching for isn't traffic — it's the ratio of email captures to completed audits. >25% means the value shown is real enough to convert.
