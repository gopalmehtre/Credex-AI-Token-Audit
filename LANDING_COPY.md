## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**

A: It's free. No credit card, no account. You get the full audit instantly. We ask for your email after — optionally — to send you the report. If your savings are significant, a Credex advisor may reach out about discounted AI credits. That's the business model: we help you find the waste, and if you want to go further, Credex can help you buy at discount. No catch.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**

A: Every number traces to an official vendor pricing page, with the date we verified it. AI tool pricing changes frequently — we re-verify weekly. If you find a discrepancy, email us and we'll update within 24 hours. The sources are listed publicly in our PRICING_DATA file on GitHub.

**Q: Does this require connecting to my billing accounts?**

A: No. You enter your spend manually. This keeps it simple, private, and fast. The trade-off is that the accuracy depends on what you enter — so use your actual invoice numbers, not rough estimates.

**Q: What happens with my data?**

A: Your audit is stored so it can be shared via a unique URL (without identifying information in the public version). Your email, if you provide it, is used to send the report and — if your savings are above $500/month — may be shared with a Credex advisor. It's never sold or shared with third parties beyond that.

**Q: What if my spend is already optimal?**

A: We'll tell you that honestly. "You're spending well" is a valid and useful audit result. We'd rather give you an accurate green light than manufacture savings to look impressive. You can sign up for alerts if pricing changes make new savings available.
